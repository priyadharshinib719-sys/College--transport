About the Portal

The College Transport Information Portal is a simple, student-friendly dashboard that brings together everything you need to plan your commute — bus numbers, routes, starting points, destinations, pickup points and timings — all in one place.

  • Real-time search across buses, routes and pickup points.
  • Filter buses by route and live status.
  • View the complete stop-by-stop route for any bus.
  • Light and dark mode that remembers your choice.
  • Fully responsive — works on phones, tablets and desktops.
